## 🚀 Flagship Themed Attraction & CAD Mechanism Builds

### 1. 🪐 Smoke & Starlight: Trackless Dark Ride AGV
* **Story & Concept:** Omnidirectional trackless ride vehicle exploring the astronomical observatory of Professor Elizabeth Starling.
* **Engineering Highlights:** Dual independent swerve-drive steer pods for 360° omnidirectional translation and yaw rotation; Python kinematic simulation (NumPy/Matplotlib) for wheel velocity and jerk envelopes compliant with ASTM F24 passenger containment limits; SolidWorks chassis FEA stress analysis.

### 2. ⏳ Time Machine Turntable & Rotary Switch Track
* **Story & Concept:** Subterranean show action switch track rotating ride vehicles 180° during a synchronized vertical descent transition.
* **Engineering Highlights:** Internal-geared slewing ring bearing driven by high-torque servo reduction; pneumatic tapered shot-pin indexing lock for sub-millimeter rail repeatability; MATLAB inertia calculations and S-curve motion profiling; FMEA fail-safe brake interlocks.

### 3. 🏴‍☠️ The Smugglers Mine Train: Articulated Coaster Bogie
* **Story & Concept:** 18th-century smuggler runaway mine train coaster vehicle.
* **Engineering Highlights:** 3-wheel bogie cluster (road, side-friction, and upstop wheels) with dual-axis spherical knuckle joints for tight mountain curves; anti-rollback dogs and chain lift hitch; EN 13814 / ASTM F24 dynamic load factor analysis ($4.0\text{G}$ vertical, $1.5\text{G}$ lateral).

## 🏛️ University & Transit Infrastructure Projects

* **🦾 Assistive Mechanical Reaching Aid (1st Year Uni Task):** SolidWorks parametric CAD assembly, custom gripper jaw geometry, laser-cut MDF physical prototyping, and workshop destructive load testing to directly observe failure modes and stress concentrations.
* **🚇 Platform Accessibility Scissor Lift:** Hydraulic transit platform lift mechanism engineered in the context of London Underground & DLR step-free accessibility.
* **🚪 Rolling Stock Pneumatic Door Dampening:** Elastomer dampening and kinetic energy absorption for passenger train door closing cycles.
* **⚙️ 2-Stage Industrial Speed Reducer Gearbox:** AGMA tooth bending stress calculations and split-casing CAD design.

## 🌐 Deploying to GitHub Pages (Live in 30 Seconds)

1. Push this repository to your GitHub account (e.g. `https://github.com/your-username/portfolio`).
2. On GitHub, navigate to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
3. Under **Build and deployment** $\rightarrow$ **Branch**, select `main` (root `/`) and click **Save**.
4. Your website will be live globally at `https://your-username.github.io/portfolio`!
